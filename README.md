One-line patch note for a bug in real life: the brain replaying your most
embarrassing moment from 2011 at 3 a.m. with no skip button and full
surround sound.

```
v2.1.7: fixed a bug where the brain replayed your most embarrassing moment from 2011 at 3 a.m. with no skip button and full surround sound.
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
